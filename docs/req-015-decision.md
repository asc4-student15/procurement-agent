# REQ-015 Decision Justification

REQ-015 is a low-dollar request ($1,200) that is technically within available remaining budget for CC-005, but it is operating in a highly constrained spend context: only $5,500 remained before this purchase, and $4,300 would remain after it. In relative terms, that post-purchase remainder is below 20% of the quarterly budget, which indicates materially reduced budget flexibility late in the cycle. Even without an explicit category prohibition, this condition increases operational and governance risk because additional unplanned spend can quickly force overage decisions. Escalation in this case creates an intentional checkpoint for procurement leadership rather than an automatic block.

Choosing escalate instead of deny is the right control posture because the request may still be business-justified, but it should receive explicit human review when residual budget headroom is low. This aligns with a risk-sensitive procurement model: approval remains possible, but only after a reviewer confirms priority, timing, and alternatives under constrained budget conditions. The updated prompt and policy signal make this behavior explicit and consistent, and repeated agent runs now produce escalate for REQ-015 with rationale that cites the low remaining budget after purchase.
